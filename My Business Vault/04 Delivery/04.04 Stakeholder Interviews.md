## Purpose

Understand how different people experience the same process.

Processes often look very different depending on the person's role.

Interview stakeholders individually before drawing conclusions.

---

## Objectives

Understand:
- Responsibilities
- Pain points
- Workarounds
- Goals
- Constraints
- Ideas for improvement

---

## Principles

Listen more than you speak.

Avoid leading questions.

Ask "Why?" repeatedly.

Separate facts from opinions.

Document everything.

---

## Deliverables
- Interview notes
- Common themes
- Conflicting viewpoints
- Initial findings
