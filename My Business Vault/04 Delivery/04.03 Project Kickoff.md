## Objectives

Before work begins, establish a shared understanding of the project.

Confirm:
- Business objectives
- Scope
- Stakeholders
- Timeline
- Communication expectations
- Success criteria

---

## Questions

- Why is this project important?
- What happens if nothing changes?
- What does success look like?
- Who makes decisions?
- Who performs the work?
- What constraints exist?

---

## Deliverables

- Kickoff meeting notes
- Stakeholder list
- Communication plan
- Project timeline
- Success metrics
