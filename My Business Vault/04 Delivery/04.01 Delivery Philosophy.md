Every engagement should reduce uncertainty.

Clients should always understand:

- what we are doing,
- why we are doing it,
- what comes next,
- and how success will be measured.

Our role is to create clarity throughout the engagement.

Documentation is not an afterthought.

It is one of the primary deliverables.

Whenever practical:
- Show your work.
- Validate assumptions.
- Communicate frequently.
- Keep stakeholders informed.
- Leave the organization more capable than before.
