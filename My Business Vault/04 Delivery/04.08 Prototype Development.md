## Purpose

Reduce implementation risk through experimentation.

A prototype answers questions before significant investment is made.

---

## Examples

- Python scripts
- Dashboard prototypes
- Workflow automation
- AI assistants
- Data models
- Report automation

---

## Principles

Build quickly.

Validate assumptions.

Collect feedback.

Iterate.

Discard ideas that do not create value.

---

## Deliverables

- Working prototype
- Validation results
- User feedback
- Recommendations
