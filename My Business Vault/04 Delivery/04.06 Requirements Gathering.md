## Purpose

Clearly define what the solution must accomplish.

Requirements should describe business needs—not implementation details.

---

## Categories
- Functional requirements
- Non-functional requirements
- Business rules
- Assumptions
- Constraints
- Success criteria

---

## Principles

Requirements should be:
- Clear
- Testable
- Necessary
- Traceable

Avoid vague language.

Every requirement should exist for a business reason.

---

## Deliverables

- Requirements document
- Assumption log
- Open questions
