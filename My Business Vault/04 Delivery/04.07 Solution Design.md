## Purpose

Translate business requirements into practical solutions.

Solutions should support business processes rather than forcing the business to change unnecessarily.

---

## Activities

- Workflow design
- Data flow design
- System interactions
- KPI definition
- Risk identification
- Trade-off evaluation

---

## Principles

Prefer simple solutions.

Avoid unnecessary customization.

Design for maintainability.

Technology should reduce complexity.

---

## Deliverables

- Solution overview
- Workflow diagrams
- Data flow diagrams
- Implementation roadmap
