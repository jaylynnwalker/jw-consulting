## Purpose

Visualize how work actually flows through the organization.

Process maps should expose:
- Decision points
- Delays
- Hand-offs
- Redundant work
- Information flow

---

## Principles

Map the current state before designing the future state.

Keep diagrams simple.

Focus on understanding rather than perfection.

Validate maps with stakeholders.

---

## Deliverables

- Current-state process map
- Pain point annotations
- Improvement opportunities
- Questions for further investigation
