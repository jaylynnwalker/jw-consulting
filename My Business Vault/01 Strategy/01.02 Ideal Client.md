## Primary Client

Small to medium manufacturers that have outgrown their existing processes but are not yet large enough to maintain dedicated business analysis or digital transformation teams.

Typical characteristics:
- 50–500 employees
- Engineering-focused
- Limited internal IT resources
- Multiple disconnected systems
- Heavy spreadsheet usage
- Growing operations
- Leadership open to change

---

## Industries

Primary:

- Specialty Chemicals
- Polymers
- Plastics
- Coatings
- Adhesives
- Composites

Secondary:

- Food Manufacturing
- Industrial Materials
- Consumer Products
- Other process manufacturing

- Owner

- Operations Manager

## Problems They Experience

- Manual reporting
- Duplicate data entry
- Poor visibility
- Inconsistent processes
- Spreadsheet dependence
- Software frustration
- Lack of trustworthy KPIs
- Unclear requirements for technology projects
