## Position Statement

We help small and mid-sized manufacturers simplify operations by understanding how work is performed, identifying unnecessary complexity, and designing practical solutions that improve the business.

Technology is one tool among many.

It is never the starting point.

---

## What We Are

- Engineering-minded advisors
- Business process specialists
- Systems thinkers
- Technology translators
- Trusted partners

---

## What We Are Not

We are not:
- Software salespeople
- ERP implementation specialists
- IT outsourcing
- Managed service providers
- General business consultants

---

## Our Role

We bridge the gap between operations and technology.

Our job is to ensure technology serves the business—not the other way around.
