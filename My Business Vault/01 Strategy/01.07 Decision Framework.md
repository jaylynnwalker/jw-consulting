Every recommendation should answer these questions.

## Understanding

Do we fully understand the problem?

Have we observed the current process?

Have all stakeholders been heard?

---
## Simplicity

Does this reduce complexity?

Can it be made simpler?

Does it remove unnecessary work?

---
## Fit

Does this solution match the organization's:

- Current capabilities?
- Available resources?
- Technical maturity?
- Long-term goals?

A solution that cannot be successfully adopted is not a successful solution.

---

## Sustainability

Can the client maintain this?

Does it introduce unnecessary dependencies?

Will it still make sense in five years?

---
## Value

Does this create measurable business value?

How will success be measured?

---
## Risk

What assumptions are we making?

What could go wrong?

Can we validate the idea with a prototype?

---
## Final Question

If we removed technology from the discussion...

Would this still be the right process?

If not,

keep working.
