## Purpose

Design simpler, more effective ways of working before introducing new technology.

Process redesign focuses on improving the business first.

Technology follows.

---

## Typical Activities

- Current-state review
- Future-state workshops
- Process simplification
- Responsibility clarification
- Workflow redesign
- KPI definition

---

## Deliverables

- Future-state process maps
- Process documentation
- Decision points
- Roles and responsibilities
- KPI recommendations

---

## Success Criteria

Processes become:
- simpler
- easier to understand
- easier to measure
- and easier to improve
