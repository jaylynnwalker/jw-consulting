## Purpose

Understand how the organization operates today and identify opportunities to simplify processes, improve visibility, and reduce unnecessary complexity.

This service is often the first engagement with a client.

---

## Typical Activities

- Site observations
- Stakeholder interviews
- Process mapping
- System inventory
- Data flow review
- Pain point analysis

---

## Deliverables

- Executive summary
- Current-state process maps
- Key observations
- Risks and bottlenecks
- Improvement opportunities
- Prioritized recommendations

---

## Typical Client Questions

- Why is this process so slow?
- Where are our biggest inefficiencies?
- Why are we constantly working around our systems?
- What should we improve first?

---

## Success Criteria

The client leaves with a clear understanding of:

- how work actually flows,
- where complexity exists,
- and which improvements will create the greatest impact.
