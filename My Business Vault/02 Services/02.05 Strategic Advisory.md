## Purpose

Provide targeted guidance to organizations making complex operational and technology decisions.

Strategic advisory is designed to provide expertise and perspective without creating long-term dependency.

Our role is to help clients make better decisions, not become a permanent member of their team.

---

## Example Topics

- Technology roadmap decisions
- Process improvement priorities
- Vendor evaluation
- AI opportunity assessment
- Manufacturing analytics strategy
- Digital improvement planning

---

## Engagement Models

Strategic advisory is typically structured as:

- Defined advisory projects
- Short-term consulting support
- Periodic review sessions
- Follow-up assessments after completed engagements

---

## Boundaries

We do not aim to become an embedded operational resource.

Successful consulting should leave the organization more capable and confident.

Our role is to:

- Provide expertise.
- Challenge assumptions.
- Clarify options.
- Support important decisions.

The client owns the ongoing operation and improvement of their business.

---

## Success Criteria

The client:

- Makes better-informed decisions.
- Has clarity on next steps.
- Can move forward independently.
- Knows they have a trusted resource available for future challenges.
