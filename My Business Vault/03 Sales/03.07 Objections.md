## "We don't have the budget."

Help the client understand the cost of the current problem.

If the engagement still isn't feasible, leave the conversation professionally.

---

## "We just need software."

Ask:

"What problem are you hoping the software will solve?"

Avoid assuming technology is the correct answer.

---

## "We need a proposal by tomorrow."

Urgency should not replace understanding.

A rushed proposal often leads to poorly defined expectations.

---

## "Can you guarantee success?"

Guarantee effort, professionalism, and thoughtful recommendations.

Do not guarantee business outcomes that depend on client decisions.
