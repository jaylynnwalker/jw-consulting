Every proposal should answer four questions.

## 1. What problem are we solving?

State the business problem clearly.

---

## 2. Why does it matter?

Explain the business impact.

Avoid technical language when possible.

---

## 3. What will we do?

Define:
- Scope
- Deliverables
- Timeline
- Responsibilities

---

## 4. What does success look like?

Success should be measurable whenever possible.

Examples include:
- Reduced reporting effort.
- Improved process visibility.
- Better defined requirements.
- Increased process consistency.
