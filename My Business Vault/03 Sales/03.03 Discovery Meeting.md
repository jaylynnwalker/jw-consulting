## Purpose

Understand the business before discussing solutions.

The meeting should focus on the client—not on our services.

---

## Objectives

Understand:
- Business goals
- Current processes
- Existing systems
- Frustrations
- Desired outcomes
- Constraints
- Decision makers

---

## Avoid
- Recommending software
- Offering solutions too early
- Estimating effort during the meeting
- Making commitments before understanding the problem

---

## Success

A successful discovery meeting ends with:
- Clear understanding of the client's situation.
- Agreement on next steps.
- Mutual confidence that further discussion is worthwhile.
