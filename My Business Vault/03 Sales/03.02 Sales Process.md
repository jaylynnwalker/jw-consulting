## Step 1 - Initial Contact

Understand why the client reached out.

The objective is not to solve the problem.

The objective is to determine whether additional conversations are worthwhile.

---
## Step 2 - Discovery Meeting

Meet with key stakeholders.

Listen more than you speak.

Ask questions.

Understand goals, frustrations, and constraints.

---
## Step 3 - Internal Evaluation

After the meeting, determine:
- Can we create value?
- Is this within our expertise?
- Do we have the capacity?
- Does the engagement align with our philosophy?

Not every opportunity should become a proposal.

---

## Step 4 - Proposal

Clearly define:
- Scope
- Objectives
- Deliverables
- Timeline
- Assumptions
- Pricing

Avoid vague language.

---

## Step 5 - Engagement

Begin the consulting methodology.

Observe before recommending.

---

## Step 6 - Relationship

Continue providing value after project completion.

Long-term relationships are preferred over one-time engagements.
