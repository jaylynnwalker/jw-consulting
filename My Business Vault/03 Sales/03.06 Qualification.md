Not every project is a good fit.

Before preparing a proposal, ask:
- Does the client have a real business problem?
- Can we create meaningful value?
- Is the client willing to change?
- Are decision makers engaged?
- Is the expected scope appropriate?
- Can success be defined?

If the answer to several of these questions is "no," politely decline the opportunity.

Protecting the firm's reputation is more important than increasing utilization.
