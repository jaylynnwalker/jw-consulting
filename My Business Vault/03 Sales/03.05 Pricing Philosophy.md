Price should reflect value created—not hours worked.

Whenever practical, use fixed-fee engagements with clearly defined deliverables.

Examples include:
- Operational Assessment
- Process Redesign
- Solution Design

For work with evolving scope, use time-and-materials or advisory retainers.

Avoid discounting to win business.

If a client values only the lowest price, they are unlikely to value thoughtful consulting.

Our objective is to build long-term relationships based on trust and measurable outcomes.
