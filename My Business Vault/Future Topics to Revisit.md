# Financial Planning

## Revenue Model

- Build a five-year financial model.
- Determine target annual income.
- Estimate consulting utilization.
- Establish pricing for each service offering.
- Define revenue milestones for transitioning to full-time consulting.

## Taxes
- Meet with a CPA.
- Determine whether and when to elect S-Corporation taxation.
- Plan for estimated quarterly tax payments.

---

# Legal

## Business Formation

- Form a Texas LLC before accepting the first paying client.
- Obtain an EIN.
- Open a dedicated business bank account.

## Contracts

Work with a business attorney to develop:

- Master Services Agreement (MSA)
- Statement of Work (SOW)
- Mutual NDA
- Independent Contractor Agreement (if needed)

Review client-provided agreements carefully, especially clauses related to:

- Intellectual property
- Liability
- Indemnification
- Confidentiality
- Insurance requirements

## Intellectual Property

Determine how to protect:

- Consulting methodology
- Templates
- Python utilities
- AI prompts
- Frameworks
- Other reusable intellectual property

Evaluate whether a trademark for the business name is worthwhile after the business is established.

# Consulting Philosophy

Continue refining principles as experience grows.

Current ideas to reinforce:

- Fit over complexity.
    
- Build capability, not dependency.
