## 10-Year Goals

- Operate a respected boutique consulting practice.
- Maintain fewer than 10 active clients.
- Build long-term relationships rather than one-time projects.
- Be recognized for thoughtful problem solving.
- Continue learning every year.
- Maintain excellent work-life balance.

---

## What Success Looks Like

Success is not measured by revenue alone.

Success means:
- Clients trust my recommendations.
- My work improves how organizations operate.
- I enjoy solving meaningful problems.
- I have time for family, travel, gardening, and personal projects.
- I remain curious.
