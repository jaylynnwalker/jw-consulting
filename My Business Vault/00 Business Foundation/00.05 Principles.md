## 1. Observe before acting.

Do not begin with assumptions.

Begin with evidence.

---

## 2. Understand the business before the technology.

Software exists to support the business.

Never reverse this relationship.

---

## 3. Simplicity scales.

Complex solutions create future problems.

Simple systems are easier to maintain, teach, and improve.

---

## 4. Build only what creates value.

Avoid unnecessary features.

Avoid unnecessary reports.

Avoid unnecessary meetings.

Avoid unnecessary technology.

---

## 5. Make information visible.

People make better decisions when information is accessible, timely, and understandable.

---

## 6. Leave clients better than you found them.

Clients should become more capable—not more dependent.

Knowledge transfer is part of every engagement.

---

## 7. Solve root causes.

Temporary fixes create future work.

Understand why problems exist before proposing solutions.

---

## 8. Continuous improvement is never finished.

Every improvement creates opportunities for another.

---
## 9. Fit Over Complexity

The best solution is not always the most advanced solution.

Solutions should be selected based on the organization's needs, capabilities, resources, and goals.

We do not recommend technology because it is new, popular, or considered best-in-class.

We recommend solutions that create meaningful value and can be successfully adopted.
The right solution is the one that fits the business.

---
## 10. Build Capability, Not Dependency

Our goal is not to become a permanent resource within a client's organization.

We help clients understand problems, design solutions, and develop the capability to sustain improvements independently.

A successful engagement reduces the client's dependence on external support while creating trust for future collaboration.
