## Purpose

Define the path from employment to independent consulting.

The transition should reduce unnecessary risk.

---

## Phase 1: Exploration

Focus:

- Build expertise
- Develop methodology
- Create portfolio materials
- Strengthen network

---

## Phase 2: First Clients

Focus:

- Small engagements
- Referrals
- Testing services
- Learning delivery

---

## Phase 3: Part-Time Consulting

Focus:

- Establish repeatable revenue
- Build confidence
- Understand demand

---

## Phase 4: Full-Time Transition

Consider transition when:

- Revenue is consistent
- Client pipeline exists
- Financial runway is available
- Consulting work is sustainable

---

## Principle

Do not leave employment because of frustration.

Leave because the consulting practice has earned the opportunity.
