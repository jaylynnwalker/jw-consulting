## Engagement Types

## Assessment Engagements

Purpose:

Help clients understand their current situation.

Examples:

- Process assessment
- Digital opportunity assessment
- Manufacturing workflow review

Characteristics:

- Lower risk for client
- Entry point for new relationships
- Fixed scope

---

## Improvement Projects

Purpose:

Design and validate improvements.

Examples:

- Process redesign
- Automation prototypes
- Solution design

Characteristics:

- Higher value
- More involved engagement
- Fixed fee preferred

---

## Advisory Retainers

Purpose:

Provide ongoing strategic support.

Examples:

- Monthly consulting support
- Technology decisions
- Continuous improvement guidance

Characteristics:

- Predictable revenue
- Long-term relationships
- Limited client load

---

## Preferred Mix

The business should gradually move toward:

- Fewer project-only clients
- More recurring advisory relationships
