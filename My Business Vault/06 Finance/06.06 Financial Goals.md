## Early Stage

Primary goal:

Validate demand.

Success means:

- First paying client
- Positive client feedback
- Repeat opportunities
- Referrals

---

## Growth Stage

Primary goal:

Create consistency.

Success means:

- Multiple active clients
- Predictable revenue
- Repeatable delivery process

---

## Mature Stage

Primary goal:

Maintain quality and flexibility.

Success means:

- Strong reputation
- Sustainable workload
- Financial independence
- Selective client relationships

---

## Definition of Success

Success is not becoming the largest consulting firm.

Success is building a respected practice that provides meaningful work and a sustainable lifestyle.
