## Principle

A successful consulting business does not require hundreds of clients.

A small number of strong relationships can create a sustainable practice.

---

## Financial Priorities

The company prioritizes:

1. Sustainable profitability
2. Client value
3. Quality of work
4. Personal flexibility
5. Long-term relationships

Revenue growth is valuable only when it supports these priorities.

---

## Avoid

The company avoids:

- Growing faster than systems allow
- Taking projects that do not align with expertise
- Competing primarily on price
- Maximizing utilization at the expense of quality
- Creating unnecessary overhead

---

## Goal

Build a business that is valuable because of expertise, judgment, and trust.
