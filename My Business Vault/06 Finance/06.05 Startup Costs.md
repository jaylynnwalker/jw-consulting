The business should remain intentionally low overhead.

---

## Initial Expenses

Potential costs:

- Business registration
- Insurance
- Website/domain
- Accounting software
- Professional software
- Marketing materials
- Professional memberships

---

## Avoid Early

Avoid unnecessary expenses:

- Office space
- Employees
- Expensive software subscriptions
- Large marketing campaigns

---

## Principle

Invest in credibility and capability.

Do not invest in appearance before demand exists.
