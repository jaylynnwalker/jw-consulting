# Pricing Strategy

## Principle

Pricing should reflect the value of better decisions, not only the time required.

Poor decisions in manufacturing can create significant costs.

The value of reducing uncertainty is substantial.

---

## Preferred Pricing Models

## Fixed Fee

Best for:

- Assessments
- Process mapping
- Solution design
- Defined prototypes

Benefits:

- Clear expectations
- Rewards efficiency
- Easier budgeting

---

## Advisory Retainer

Best for:

- Ongoing guidance
- Strategic support
- Long-term relationships

Benefits:

- Predictable revenue
- Deeper client understanding

---

## Hourly Billing

Used selectively.

Appropriate for:

- Short advisory requests
- Undefined scope
- Additional support outside agreed work

Hourly billing should not be the primary value proposition.
