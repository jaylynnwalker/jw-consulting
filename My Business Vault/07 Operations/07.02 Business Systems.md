## Purpose

Define the core systems required to operate the business.

---

## Core Systems

### Client Management

Used for:

- Contact information
- Relationship tracking
- Opportunity management
- Follow-ups

---

### Project Management

Used for:

- Scope tracking
- Milestones
- Tasks
- Deliverables
- Timelines

---

### Financial Management

Used for:

- Invoicing
- Expenses
- Revenue tracking
- Financial reporting

---

### Knowledge Management

Used for:

- Methodology
- Templates
- Research
- Lessons learned

---

## Principle

Use the simplest system that supports the current size of the business.

Do not build enterprise processes for a small firm.
