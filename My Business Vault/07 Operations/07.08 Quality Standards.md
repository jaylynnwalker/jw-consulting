## Purpose

Define what excellent work looks like.

---

## Every Engagement Should:

- Start with understanding.
- Clearly define the problem.
- Include stakeholder input.
- Avoid unnecessary complexity.
- Provide practical recommendations.
- Document decisions.

---

## Recommendations Should Be:

- Realistic
- Sustainable
- Appropriate for the client
- Connected to business value

---

## Final Test

Would this recommendation still make sense if technology was not the focus?

If not, reconsider.
