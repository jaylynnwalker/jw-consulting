## Purpose

Protect the sustainability of the business.

A successful consulting practice should improve quality of life, not recreate corporate burnout.

---

## Principles

Avoid:

- Becoming permanently embedded with clients.
- Maintaining constant availability.
- Accepting every opportunity.
- Growing faster than desired.

---

## Protect

Prioritize:

- Focused work time
- Personal flexibility
- Learning
- Health
- Relationships

---

## Definition of Success

The business should provide meaningful work while allowing control over time and priorities.
