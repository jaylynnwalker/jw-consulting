# Time Management

## Purpose

Protect time for high-value work.

Consulting businesses can easily become dominated by meetings and administration.

---

## Priority Activities

Highest value:

- Client problem solving
- Process analysis
- Solution design
- Prototype development
- Learning

---

## Lower Value Activities

Minimize:

- Administrative tasks
- Repetitive reporting
- Unnecessary meetings
- Manual processes

---

## Scheduling Principles

Reserve time for:

- Deep work
- Client communication
- Business development
- Professional development

Avoid allowing every available hour to become client work.
