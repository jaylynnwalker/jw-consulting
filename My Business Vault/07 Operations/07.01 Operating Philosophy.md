The company should operate with the same principles used when helping clients.

Observe.

Understand.

Simplify.

Improve.

---

## Simplicity

A small consulting firm should remain intentionally simple.

Complexity should only be added when it creates meaningful value.

---

## Quality Over Volume

The goal is not to maximize the number of clients.

The goal is to produce excellent work for clients where we can create meaningful impact.

---

## Independence

The business should support professional independence.

Systems should reduce administrative burden and preserve time for valuable work.

---

## Continuous Improvement

The company should regularly evaluate:

- What is working?
- What creates unnecessary effort?
- What can be simplified?
