## Purpose

Maintain professional relationships while avoiding unhealthy dependency.

---

## Principles

Clients should view us as:

- A trusted expert
- A problem-solving partner
- A source of clarity

Not:

- An internal employee
- A permanent resource
- The owner of their processes

---

## Engagement Boundaries

Maintain clarity around:

- Scope
- Availability
- Responsibilities
- Decision ownership

---

## Goal

Create relationships where clients return because they value expertise, not because they cannot operate without us.
