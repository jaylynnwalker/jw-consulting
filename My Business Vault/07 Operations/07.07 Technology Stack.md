## Principle

Technology should support the business, not become the business.

Use tools that improve organization, communication, and efficiency.

---

## Categories

Potential tools:

### Knowledge Management

- Notes
- Documentation
- Research

### Communication

- Email
- Video meetings
- Client collaboration

### Project Management

- Tasks
- Timelines
- Deliverables

### Development

- Python
- Data tools
- Automation tools

### Finance

- Accounting
- Invoicing
- Expense tracking

---

## Selection Criteria

Choose tools based on:

- Simplicity
- Reliability
- Cost
- Integration
- Long-term usefulness
