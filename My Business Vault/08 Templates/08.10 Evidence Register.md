## Purpose

Document the observations, interviews, measurements, and supporting information that led to project findings and recommendations.

Recommendations should be supported by evidence whenever possible.

This register improves transparency and increases confidence in consulting recommendations.

---

## Sources of Evidence

Examples include:

- Site observations
- Stakeholder interviews
- Process maps
- Existing documentation
- System screenshots
- Production data
- KPI analysis
- Workshop outcomes
- Historical reports

---

## Standard Fields

### Observation ID

Unique identifier.

---

### Date

When the evidence was collected.

---

### Source

Where the information originated.

Examples:

- Maintenance Manager
- Production Supervisor
- SAP Report
- PI Historian
- Plant Walkthrough

---

### Observation

Objective description of what was observed.

Avoid interpretation.

---

### Supporting Files

Reference diagrams, screenshots, reports, or datasets.

---

### Business Impact

Explain why the observation matters.

---

### Related Finding

Link to the consulting finding supported by this evidence.

---

### Related Recommendation

Link to the recommendation influenced by this evidence.

---

## Example

Observation:

Operators manually copy production numbers into Excel every shift.

Source:

Plant observation

Business Impact:

Introduces transcription errors and delays reporting.

Supports Finding:

Manual reporting process creates unnecessary administrative effort.

Supports Recommendation:

Automate production reporting using historian data.
