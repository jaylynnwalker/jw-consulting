## Purpose

Capture important decisions made throughout an engagement.

A decision log creates transparency, preserves organizational knowledge, and provides future teams with the reasoning behind recommendations.

Good consulting should help clients remember not only *what* was decided, but *why*.

---

## Benefits

- Reduces repeated discussions.
- Documents trade-offs.
- Supports executive decision making.
- Creates accountability.
- Preserves knowledge after the project ends.

---

## Standard Fields

### Decision

A concise description of the decision.

---

### Background

Why this decision became necessary.

---

### Options Considered

Summarize the viable alternatives.

Include advantages and disadvantages where appropriate.

---

### Recommendation

The consultant's recommended approach.

---

### Decision Status

- Proposed
- Approved
- Deferred
- Rejected
- Implemented

---

### Decision Owner

Who is responsible for approving or implementing the decision.

---

### Business Impact

Expected benefits.

Examples:

- Reduced manual effort
- Improved data quality
- Lower operational risk
- Faster reporting
- Improved visibility

---

### Dependencies

Anything that must occur before implementation.

---

### Review Date

If appropriate, identify when the decision should be revisited.

---

## Example

Decision:
Standardize production KPI definitions across all departments.

Background:
Operations, Finance, and Engineering currently calculate OEE differently.

Options Considered:

- Maintain department-specific KPIs
- Standardize enterprise KPI definitions

Recommendation:

Create one enterprise KPI standard governed by Operations.

Expected Benefits:

- Consistent reporting
- Better executive dashboards
- Improved AI readiness

Status:

Approved

Owner:

Operations Manager
