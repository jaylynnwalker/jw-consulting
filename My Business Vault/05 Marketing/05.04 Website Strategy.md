# Website Strategy

## Purpose

The website should establish credibility and make it easy for potential clients to understand:

- Who we help.
- What problems we solve.
- How we work.
- Why we are different.

---

## Primary Audience

- Plant managers
- Operations leaders
- Manufacturing executives
- Engineering managers

---

## Website Principles

The website should:

- Explain problems before services.
- Avoid technical jargon.
- Demonstrate manufacturing understanding.
- Emphasize outcomes.

---

### Homepage

Simplify Complexity.

### About

Engineering background.

How we help.

### Approach

### Insights

Articles and examples.

### Contact

Conversation, not sales pitch.

---
## Case Studies

Case studies should be publicly available whenever possible.

The purpose is not lead capture.

The purpose is to demonstrate how we think and how we approach problems.

Case studies should focus on:

- The business problem.
- The process being improved.
- The reasoning behind recommendations.
- The solution approach.
- The measurable outcome.

Case studies should educate potential clients before the first conversation.
