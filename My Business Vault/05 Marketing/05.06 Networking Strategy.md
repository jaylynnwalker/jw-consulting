## Principle

The first clients will likely come through relationships, not advertising.

---

## Priority Networks

- Former manufacturing colleagues
- Chemical industry contacts
- Houston manufacturing community
- Professional organizations
- Industry events
- Engineering networks

---

## Relationship Approach

Do not begin conversations by selling.

Begin by:

- Asking about challenges.
- Sharing useful information.
- Learning about their business.

---

## Long-Term Goal

Become someone people contact when they encounter operational challenges.
